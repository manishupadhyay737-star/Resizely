Common questions

What people usually ask before resizing their first image.

Is Resizely really free?

Yes. Resizing and compressing images costs nothing, has no watermark, and doesn't require an account.

Do my images get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every resize happens on your own device using your browser's canvas engine — files never touch a server.

Which formats are supported?

You can resize and compress JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF files, and convert between JPG, PNG and WebP on export.

What size should I use for social media or email?

1200×1200px at 80–85% quality works well for social posts; 1900×1100px at 90% quality is a safe, sharp size for email or messaging.
