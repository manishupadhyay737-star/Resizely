Privacy Policy

This policy explains the current privacy approach for Resizely.

Image processing

The core Resizely image workflow is designed to process selected images locally in the browser. The website does not need to upload image pixels to a Resizely processing server to perform the core resize and compression functions.

Information you provide

The core image tool does not require an account. If you contact the site owner, information you voluntarily include in your message may be received for the purpose of responding to you.

Cookies and analytics

If analytics, advertising, cookies or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to describe those services and the information they process.

Third-party services

The website may use infrastructure or third-party resources required to deliver the site. Users should review the applicable privacy information of those providers where relevant.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website's features, analytics, advertising or infrastructure change.
