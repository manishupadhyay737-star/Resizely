Privacy Policy

Resizely is designed so the core image-processing workflow can run locally in your browser.

Images

When you use the image tool, selected image data is processed by your browser for resizing, compression and conversion. The core workflow does not require uploading the image to a Resizely server.

Website analytics and third parties

If analytics, advertising or other third-party services are added to Resizely in the future, their applicable cookies, local storage and data practices will be disclosed here and in the relevant service documentation.

Changes

This policy may be updated when website features or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page.
